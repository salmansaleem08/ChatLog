# ChatLog

ChatLog is a workspace for small businesses that sell through WhatsApp. Owners get a constant stream of customer messages—orders with different products, colors, sizes, and delivery details—but chat apps do not offer a real order book, so lines get missed, stock is tracked in notebooks or spreadsheets, and profit is mostly guesswork.

ChatLog turns those conversations into structured orders: who ordered what, which variant, how many, at what price, and where it should go. Each customer is tied to their phone number, and when an order is finished, the next message from that chat starts a clean new order so old jobs never mix with new ones.

From there you confirm or adjust orders, move them through a clear status path, and keep customers in the loop with messages that match each stage. Inventory—including variants—updates as orders move and can be restored when something is returned. Analytics show revenue, costs, profit, popular products, returns, and how your business is trending over time, so decisions are based on data instead of memory.

ChatLog is built for independent sellers and small teams who want serious operations without enterprise complexity.
